# Gold Gerb (Ground Fountain)

`theatricalextralights:gerb_gold` · family: [Pyro & fireworks](/fixtures/pyro)

Stage gerb, a gold spark fountain.

- Carries a **safety arm**: disarmed in its screen, it reads DMX but never fires. See [Pyro & safety arm](/guide/pyro).
- Found in the **Theatrical: Pyro** creative tab.

## 2-Channel Gerb (2 ch)

| Ch | Function | Values |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Intensity | 0 off to 255 full |
| 2 | Tilt | 0 to 255 |

### Channel by channel

**1 · Intensity** — Master dimmer. 0 is dark, 255 is full output. The beam, the projected spot and the dynamic light in the room all scale with it. Fades are smooth: the client interpolates between DMX frames.

**2 · Tilt** — Angle of the fountain.
