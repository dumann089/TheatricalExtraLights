# Flame Projector

`theatricalextralights:flame_projector` · family: [Pyro & fireworks](/fixtures/pyro)

Vertical flame column.

- Carries a **safety arm**: disarmed in its screen, it reads DMX but never fires. See [Pyro & safety arm](/guide/pyro).
- Found in the **Theatrical: Pyro** creative tab.

## 2-Channel Flame Projector (2 ch)

| Ch | Function | Values |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flame length | 0 off to 255 full |
| 2 | Head angle | 0 to 255 |

### Channel by channel

**1 · Flame length** — Height of the flame and the trigger: 0 is off.

**2 · Head angle** — Tilt of the flame head.
