# Moving Panel Par64 (DWT)

`theatricalextralights:dwt_panel` · family: [Moving heads & beams](/fixtures/moving-heads)

A panning panel of four white PAR sections plus a warm section.

## 6-Channel Mode (6 ch)

| Ch | Function | Values |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Section 1 | 0 to 255 |
| 2 | Section 2 | 0 to 255 |
| 3 | Section 3 | 0 to 255 |
| 4 | Section 4 | 0 to 255 |
| 5 | Warm section | 0 to 255 |
| 6 | Pan | 0 to 255 = 0° to 360° |

### Channel by channel

**1 · Section 1** — Level of white section 1.

**2 · Section 2** — Level of white section 2.

**3 · Section 3** — Level of white section 3.

**4 · Section 4** — Level of white section 4.

**5 · Warm section** — Level of the warm-white section, tinted amber.

**6 · Pan** — Rotation of the panel over a full turn. Unlike the moving heads this range starts at 0°, not −180°.
