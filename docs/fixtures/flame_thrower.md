# Flame Thrower

`theatricalextralights:flame_thrower` · family: [Pyro & fireworks](/fixtures/pyro)

Directed flame jet.

- Carries a **safety arm**: disarmed in its screen, it reads DMX but never fires. See [Pyro & safety arm](/guide/pyro).
- Found in the **Theatrical: Pyro** creative tab.

## 2-Channel Flame Thrower (2 ch)

| Ch | Function | Values |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Flame | 0 off to 255 full |
| 2 | Pan | 0 to 255 |

### Channel by channel

**1 · Flame** — Length of the jet and the trigger.

**2 · Pan** — Horizontal direction of the jet.
