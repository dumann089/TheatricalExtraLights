# Animation Synchronizer

An item that plays recordings on several cameras at the same time.

1. Hold the **Animation Synchronizer** and use it (right click). A menu lists every camera in the world, nearest first.
2. Click a camera to choose which of its recordings it plays. Clicking again goes to the next recording and finally back to "none". Cameras left on "none" are not used. Broadcast and Jib cameras only list their own recordings (made with **R** inside the camera).
3. **Loop** repeats the recordings until stopped. **Play all** sends each camera its recording and then starts all of them in the same server tick. **Stop all** stops every camera in the list.

The cameras play on the server, so everyone sees them move. Like a recording that keeps playing after you leave the camera, they move pan / tilt, height, boom and dolly; zoom and exposure only exist in an operator's view. Getting on a camera takes control and stops its recording. The menu refreshes by itself, so cameras that are playing show a ▶.
