# Shoulder Camera

A camera for walking shots. Hold the item and the camera sits on your right shoulder, with your arm up holding it (everyone sees it).

**Right click** turns it on: you look through the camera and operate it like the Broadcast Camera. The mouse pans and tilts the camera, WASD walks, and zoom, exposure, the HUD and the recordings (**R**) all work the same. **Shift** or **Esc** turns it off.

Recordings keep your walking too (the movement is recorded like a dolly). When a recording plays on its own (after you leave the camera, or from the Animation Synchronizer) a figure with **your skin** appears and walks the recorded path carrying the camera, so the camera never floats. The figure goes away when the recording ends. Using the item again takes control back.

Each player's shoulder camera always has the same identity, so a Video Switcher key stays tied to it. While you carry the item your shoulder camera always exists (standing still on you, invisible), so it is always in the Animation Synchronizer and Video Switcher lists, marked with your name. It disappears when you leave the game or no longer carry the item. WASD walks in any direction (diagonals included), relative to where the camera looks.

Recordings remember where they were made: when a shoulder camera recording is played from the Animation Synchronizer, the figure appears at the recorded place and walks from there (in the same dimension).
